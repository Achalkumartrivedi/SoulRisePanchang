# Privacy Policy for SoulRise Panchang

**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 2, 2026  

Welcome to **SoulRise Panchang** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy while providing accurate Vedic Panchang, Tithi timings, Choghadiya, Janam Kundli, and Hindu calendar features.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide
- **Profile Name**: When you sign in as a Guest or via Google Sign-In, we store your chosen name to personalize your app experience, such as auto-filling your name in Janam Kundli (Birth Chart) generation.
- **Email Address**: If you choose to sign in via Google Sign-In or contact support/feedback, we may record your email address.
- **Feedback & File Attachments**: When you submit feedback or contact us, you may optionally attach images or screenshots (up to 2MB).

### B. Device & Usage Information
- **Location Data (GPS)**: To calculate precise sunrise, sunset, planetary positions, Rahu Kalam, and Tithi timings for your specific city, the App requests approximate (coarse) or precise (fine) location permissions. Location data is processed locally on your device for astronomical calculations and is **never sold or shared** with third-party data brokers.
- **Notification Permissions**: To deliver local OS reminders for sacred fasts, Ekadashi, Poonam, and custom alarms set by you, the App requests notification permissions.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:
1. **Astrological & Panchang Calculations**: Computing city-specific Panchang, Rahu Kalam, and Kundli charts.
2. **Personalization**: Pre-populating your name in Janam Kundli calculations and greeting you in the app.
3. **Local Reminders**: Triggering local OS notifications for your set reminders.
4. **Customer Support & Improvement**: Responding to customer feedback and bug reports.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage**: All user profiles, Panchang preferences, custom reminders, and Kundli charts are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage (`AsyncStorage`).
- **No Selling of Personal Data**: We **do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer** any personal data, location metrics, or user profiles to third parties.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App may utilize standard third-party libraries for core functions:
- **Google Sign-In**: Used for optional account authentication.
- **Expo Framework**: Used for cross-platform app deployment and local notification scheduling.

---

## 5. Account Deletion & Data Control

You retain full control over your data:
- **Delete Account & Reset Data**: You can wipe all stored local profiles, reminders, and app settings at any time directly within the app by navigating to **Settings ➔ Delete Account & Reset Data**.
- **Location Permissions**: You can grant or revoke location access at any time in your device settings.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SoulRise Panchang is a spiritual and educational calendar application rated **3+ / Everyone**. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
