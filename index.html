Official App Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026
App Name: SoulRise Panchang (Vedic Calendar & Kundli)

Welcome to SoulRise Panchang ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy while providing accurate Vedic Panchang, Tithi timings, Choghadiya, Janam Kundli, and Hindu calendar features.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Profile Name: When you sign in as a Guest or via Google Sign-In, we store your chosen name to personalize your app experience, such as auto-filling your name in Janam Kundli (Birth Chart) generation.
  • Email Address: If you choose to sign in via Google Sign-In or contact support/feedback, we may record your email address.
  • Feedback & Attachments: When submitting customer feedback, you may attach an optional image/file up to 2MB in size.

B. Device & Usage Information

  • Location Data (GPS): To calculate precise sunrise, sunset, planetary positions, Rahu Kalam, and Tithi timings for your city, the App requests location permissions. Location data is processed locally on your device and is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Notification Permissions: To deliver local OS reminders for sacred fasts, Ekadashi, Poonam, and custom alarms set by you, the App requests notification permissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Computing city-specific Panchang, Rahu Kalam, and Kundli charts.
  • Pre-populating your name in Janam Kundli calculations and greeting you in the app.
  • Triggering local OS notifications for your set reminders.
  • Responding to customer feedback and support requests.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Device Storage: All user profiles, Panchang preferences, custom reminders, and Kundli charts are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage.
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any personal data, location metrics, or user profiles to third parties.

4. Account Deletion & Control

You can wipe all stored local profiles, reminders, and app settings at any time directly within the app by navigating to Settings ➔ Delete Account & Reset Data.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

SoulRise Panchang is rated 3+ / Everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Website: https://www.buildshare.in
  • In-App: Settings ➔ Send Feedback / Contact Us
