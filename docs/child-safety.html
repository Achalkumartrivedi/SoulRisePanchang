Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation (CSAE) Standards & Safety Policy

Effective Date: September 3, 2026
App Name: SoulRise Panchang (Vedic Calendar & Kundli)

SoulRise Panchang is strictly committed to protecting children and ensuring a safe digital environment. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

SoulRise Panchang strictly prohibits the creation, storage, transmission, or dissemination of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any form of child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSAE).

2. Application Scope & Architecture

  • No Public User-Generated Content (UGC): SoulRise Panchang is a utility application for personal Vedic Panchang calculations, planetary positions, and fasting reminders. It does not contain public chat rooms, direct messaging, user forums, or social networking features.
  • No User Media Sharing: Users cannot post or share images, videos, or public text visible to other users.

3. Reporting & Incident Response

If any potential child safety concern, vulnerability, or violation is brought to our attention, our designated safety team will:

  • Immediately investigate the report within 24 hours.
  • Report any instances of CSAM/CSAE directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and appropriate regional law enforcement agencies.
  • Cooperate fully with legal authorities in any investigation.
