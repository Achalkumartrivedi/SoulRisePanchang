Privacy Policy for SoulRise Panchang

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

SoulRise Panchang ("we", "our", or "app") is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application.

1. Location Data Collection & Purpose

Our app requests foreground location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) solely to calculate accurate astronomical Hindu Panchang timings, including local Sunrise, Sunset, Abhijit Muhurat, Rahu Kalam, and Choghadiya periods based on your device's geographical coordinates.

  • Location data is processed locally on your device in real-time.
  • We do not sell, track, or share your location coordinates with any third parties or advertising networks.
  • If location permission is denied, the app falls back to standard city selections without restricting core functionality.

2. Local Storage

We store app settings (such as your preferred default city and notification toggle states) locally on your device using encrypted local storage (AsyncStorage). No personal data is transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

SoulRise Panchang runs offline astronomical mathematical engines. We do not integrate third-party tracking scripts, user profiling cookies, or analytics services that harvest personal identity information.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at: support@soulrise.app
